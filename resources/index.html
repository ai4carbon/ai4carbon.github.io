🔬 Resources & Tools

A curated collection of datasets, benchmarks, and publications from the AI4Carbon Initiative and the broader research community working on machine learning for carbon cycle science.

📊 Datasets, Models & Tools

Open datasets, benchmarks and ML models from the AI4Carbon community for atmospheric transport and carbon cycle research.

CarbonBench

Benchmark dataset for Eulerian atmospheric transport models

Dataset
Learn More

FootNet

Deep-learning emulator of Lagrangian (STILT) transport footprints for high-resolution greenhouse gas flux inversions.

Transport Emulator
Paper Code

GATES

Graph-Neural-Network Atmospheric Transport Emulation System: a GNN emulator of LPDM (NAME) footprints for fast emissions inference from satellites.

Transport Emulator
Paper GitHub

FLUXCOM-X-BASE

Global NEE, GPP and ET products (2001–2020) from the FLUXCOM-X machine-learning upscaling of eddy covariance fluxes.

Flux Product
Paper Data

FLUXtrapolation

Benchmark for machine learning models that extrapolate ecosystem carbon, water and energy fluxes beyond FLUXNET towers, with temporal, spatial and temperature-based scenarios.

Benchmark
Paper

STARCOP

Hyperspectral methane plume segmentation dataset and models for point-source detection.
